How do I play?

Pick a game, earn cents, and watch your capycoins grow. Here’s everything you need.

How capycoins work

100¢ = 1 capycoin. Every game pays out in cents, and your balance is shared across all of them.

PlayPick any game on the Play page and jump in.
Earn centsClicks, jumps, catches and loot all add cents.
Collect capycoinsYour balance, last 7 days and stats live on the Dashboard.

The games

🐹Capybara ClickerClick the capybara. Every click is 5¢.
  • Controls: click or tap the capybara (or press Space or Enter).
  • Watch your clicks per second and the bar that fills toward your next capycoin.
  • 20 clicks = 1 capycoin.
Play Capybara Clicker →
🏃Capybara DashJump the thorn bushes.
  • Controls: Space, ↑, W, or tap the game to jump.
  • Every 5 seconds you survive earns 2 capycoins.
  • The game speeds up the longer you run. Small, medium and large bushes need different timing.
Play Capybara Dash →
🧺Capybara CatchCatch falling fruit in your basket.
  • Controls: move the mouse, drag on a touch screen, or use ← → (A and D).
  • Orange and strawberry are worth 1 capycoin, lemon 2, blueberry and peach 3, blackberry 4, mango 5 and grapefruit 10. Rarer fruit is worth more.
  • You have 5 lives. Miss 5 fruits and the game ends, then your final score is paid out in capycoins.
  • Fruit falls faster as your score grows.
Play Capybara Catch →
🍊Capybara StackStack oranges on the capybara’s head.
  • Controls: click, tap, Space or ↓ to drop the swinging orange.
  • Land it near the middle of the orange below. A perfect drop earns +25¢ and steadies the tower.
  • Small oranges pay 15¢ but are harder, big ones pay 6¢ but are easy, and normal ones pay 10¢. Every 5 oranges earns +1 capycoin.
  • Land too far off, or let the tower lean too far, and it topples.
Play Capybara Stack →
🎣Capybara FisherCast, wait, and reel in loot.
  • Controls: click, tap or press Space to cast.
  • Wait for the bobber to bob up and down a lot, then click again quickly to reel it in. Click too early and the fish gets spooked.
  • Everything you catch goes in your bag. Press Sell to turn it into capycoins.
  • Leaves and coins are common. Crystals, pearls, jewels and shark teeth are rare and worth the most.
Play Capybara Fisher →
🎡Daily SpinOne free spin every day.
  • Controls: press the Spin button, or Space or Enter.
  • You can spin once a day. The wheel resets at midnight, and a timer shows when your next spin is ready.
  • Prizes range from 3 to 15 capycoins. Every slice is equally likely, so small prizes show up more often.
Play Daily Spin →

Good to know

Where is my balance?

On the Dashboard (the Play page). It shows your total, your last 7 days, and what each game has earned.

Does my progress save?

Yes. Sign in and everything saves to your account: your balance, the last-7-days chart, per-game earnings, best scores and your fishing bag. Other devices pick it up when you sign in there. Playing signed out still works, and it saves to the account the next time you sign in on that device.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes. Games with sound have a small “Sound” link under the game.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes! Every game works with taps, and the pages resize to fit your screen.
